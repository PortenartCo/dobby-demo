## Brand & Style

The brand evokes the tactile, comforting, and mysterious wonder of an arcane apothecary tucked away in a cobblestone alley. It merges Victorian craftsmanship with quiet sorcery: polished mahogany shelves, bevelled glass cloches, brass mechanical dials, and warm golden candlelight flickering across aged parchment. 

The aesthetic is fundamentally **Tactile / Skeuomorphic-Editorial**:
- **Tactile Materiality:** Dense, organic surfaces reminiscent of varnished mahogany, hand-pressed rag parchment, antiqued brass bezels, and etched apothecary glass.
- **Atmospheric Warmth:** Rich, dim undertones accented by radiant amber points of light, inviting prolonged contemplation rather than sterile efficiency.
- **Artisanal Pedigree:** Hand-penned notations, ledger margins, guild insignia, and gilded edge treatments evoke bespoke craftsmanship, meticulous alchemy, and centuries of collected lore.
- **Emotional Tone:** Enigmatic yet profoundly comforting, nostalgic, scholarly, and tactile.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authentic Victorian curiosity shop under gentle candlelight:

- **Primary (`#D49B42` - Warm Amber):** Evokes illuminated potion fluids, glowing hearths, and bright brass fixtures. Serves as the primary focal color, driving primary calls to action, prominent badges, and interactive toggles.
- **Secondary (`#18332A` - Mystic Bottle Green):** Deep, herbal apothecary glass and oxidized velvet. Used for grounding secondary containers, ledger category tags, state chips, and balanced interactive surfaces.
- **Tertiary (`#E5A93C` - Gilded Brass):** Polished vintage gold used for fine filigree borders, metallic highlights, active indicator pips, and glowing candle-lit borders.
- **Neutral Palette (`#2B1B17` - Mahogany Base):**
  - **Canvas Base (`#1C120F`):** Deep shadowed timber backing the entire experience.
  - **Surface Default (`#2B1B17`):** Varnished mahogany timber boards forming panels and card containers.
  - **Surface High (`#3E2723`):** Raised cabinetry, drawers, and nested trays.
- **Parchment Accents (`#F5E6CA` & `#EDE0C8`):** Off-white parchment tones dedicated strictly to handwritten transcripts, document surfaces, and legible typographical contrasts over dark timber.
- **Text & Foreground Tones:**
  - High-emphasis labels and headers leverage Aged Parchment (`#F5E6CA`).
  - Secondary metadata adopts Muted Ochre (`#C2A584`).
  - Disabled or inactive elements recede into Weathered Slate (`#5E483F`).

## Typography

The typographical hierarchy pairs Victorian editorial elegance with warm, bookish readability:

- **Display & Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Carries the grandeur of ornate shopfront fascia, engraved brass plaques, and chapter headings in leather-bound grimoires. High contrast serifs deliver authority and historical drama.
- **Body Text (`EB Garamond`):** Emulates classic hand-set movable type on heavy fibrous paper. Provides relaxed, poetic legibility for ledger chronicles, ingredient descriptions, and historical notes.
- **Labels, Ledger Numbers & Metadata (`Literata`):** Sturdy, clear, and distinct. It provides crisp rendering for tabular figures, apothecary measures (drams, ounces), price seals, and tab navigation.
- **Stylistic Conventions:**
  - Headlines use classic title casing with tight, deliberate kerning.
  - Sub-labels and category trackers adopt tracked uppercase styling (`letterSpacing: 0.05em` to `0.08em`) resembling hot-stamped gold foil.
  - Numerals maintain proportional old-style characteristics where context allows.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors the architectural rhythm of an old shop interior: structured wooden apothecary drawers, bevelled glass display cabinets, and framed wall brackets.

- **Grid Structure:** A 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (`margin: 2.5rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`), collapsing to an 8-column layout on tablet, and a single or 4-column flow on mobile (`margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 0.75rem`).
- **Framed Cabinets & Vitrines:** Content is chunked into explicit bounded units—mimicking specimen trays and glass counter tops. Nested containers maintain balanced rhythmic margins (`space-md` internally, `space-lg` to `space-xl` externally).
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Rooted in increments of `0.25rem` (4px base), scaling deliberately to build breathing room around dense clusters of alchemical data, allowing intricate filigree dividers and brass borders to breathe without visual clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use cold, synthetic drop shadows; instead, it relies on candle-glow diffusion, inset timber carving, and layered glass vitrines:

- **Layer 0 (Workshop Floor / Backing Wall):** Deepest mahogany bark texture (`#1C120F`) with subtle vertical timber groove gradients.
- **Layer 1 (Cabinetry & Wooden Trays):** Polished mahogany surfaces (`#2B1B17`) with a 1px top border highlight (`rgba(229, 169, 60, 0.25)`) indicating light falling from above, and deep ambient bottom shadows (`0 8px 24px rgba(12, 7, 5, 0.7)`).
- **Layer 2 (Parchment Scrolls & Ardoises):** Inset or pinned documents featuring deckled edge illusions, subtle inner shadows (`inset 0 1px 3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35)`), and soft candle-lit halos.
- **Layer 3 (Floating Phials & Suspended Signs):** Elements suspended in foreground space utilize warm, amber-tinted ambient glow rings (`0 12px 32px rgba(212, 155, 66, 0.15), 0 4px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`).
- **Borders & Filigree:** Containers employ double-line borders: an outer 1px stroke of antique brass (`#E5A93C` at 30% opacity) paired with an inner 1px inset boundary, producing an authentic bevelled moulding effect.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Soft (`1`)** geometry:
- **Base Components:** Buttons, ledger rows, and inputs use a strict `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius, recalling hand-carved wood mouldings and trimmed card stock.
- **Vitrines & Cabinet Frames:** Larger card containers utilize `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem`), echoing Victorian framed glass vitrines and brass drawer handles.
- **Arched Signatures:** Hero containers, showcase banners, and feature mirrors feature arched top borders (Gothic/Romanesque bevels: `1.5rem 1.5rem 0.25rem 0.25rem`) that emulate boutique window arches and apothecary alcoves.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Primary Action (Brass Wax Seal):** Rich Amber (`#D49B42`) fill with dark mahogany text (`#1C120F`), framed by a 1px brushed brass border (`#E5A93C`). Features a subtle top gradient sheen and pressed feedback mimicking a tactile mechanical stamp.
- **Secondary Action (Timber Plank):** Dark mahogany surface (`#3E2723`) with a 1px antique brass border, illuminated by Warm Amber text (`#D49B42`). On hover, the border radiates a faint candle-glow bloom.
- **Ghost Action (Quill Inscription):** Unbordered, text-only button in Parchment Cream (`#F5E6CA`) that reveals an ornate filigree underline upon hover.

### Parchment Cards & Vitrine Trays
- **Cabinet Cards:** Crafted with dark timber backgrounds (`#2B1B17`), 1px dual-tone brass edges, and corner fleuron accents. Used for inventory grids, grimoire chapters, and apothecary drawer groupings.
- **Parchment Manuscript Cards:** Textured cream surface (`#EDE0C8`) with deep brown ink typography (`#2B1B17`), distressed deckled borders, and a faint gold foil top trim.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Recessed, drawer-slot appearance with an inset top shadow (`inset 0 2px 4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`).
- Dark walnut cavity (`#1C120F`) accented with an antique brass border when idle.
- Focus state brings a warm amber glow (`0 0 0 2px rgba(212, 155, 66, 0.4)`), with typed input appearing in vibrant Parchment Cream (`#F5E6CA`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes:** Square brass bezels (`#3E2723` background with `#E5A93C` border). Checked state reveals an engraved alchemical rune or stylized quill check mark in radiant amber.
- **Radio Buttons:** Ornate circular dials with a concentric golden brass inner dot that expands upon selection.

### Chips & Apothecary Labels
- Resemble hand-glued apothecary jar tags: aged parchment tags with clipped corners, irregular stamp borders, and faded typewriter or quill-script classifications.
- Potion rarity or category chips leverage Mystic Bottle Green (`#18332A`) or Smoked Amber (`#D49B42` with 20% opacity) with gilded 1px boundaries.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Hanging Wooden Shingle (Header Banner):** Suspended banners flanked by decorative brass chains, featuring weathered grain highlights.
- **Chalkboard Slate (Menu & Daily Tasks):** Deep charcoal matte surface (`#232425`) with rough chalk-textured typography (`#EAEAEA`) for daily meal plans and transient tasks.
- **Specimen Glass Phials (Progress Trackers):** Vertical or horizontal glass vial shapes filled with luminous, bubbling amber or green liquid indicators to visualize progress and metrics.
